Penalty

Awarded when a foul is committed inside the penalty area,giving a direct shot at goal.

Corner

Awarded when the defending team last touches the ball before it crosses the goal line(without scoring)

Foul

An illegal action such as tripping,pushing,holding,or handball that breaks the rules..

Yellow Card

A warning issued to a player for unsporting behavior or repeated fouls, promoting fair responsible conduct.
